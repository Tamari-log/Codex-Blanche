## 2. Data handled by the App (overview)

The App processes chat history, settings, API keys (if you enter them), presets, and optional Google account tokens **primarily in your browser** (`localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, etc.). **Belleval office does not operate this App as a system that uploads your chats to an operator-controlled backend for storage** (except as needed to reach third-party APIs below or optional Google Drive).

## 3. Third-party AI services (Gemini / OpenAI, etc.)

1. When you send messages, **conversation content** and text or metadata derived from attachments may be sent to the AI Provider you select.  
2. Those providers process data under **their own privacy policies and terms**.  
3. API keys may be kept in `sessionStorage` or `localStorage`, depending on settings, for authentication. Belleval office typically does not receive your keys.

## 4. Google Drive (optional)

1. If you use Drive sync, the App may use Google sign-in and the Google Drive API to read/write backup files.  
2. Google’s policies apply (for example the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

## 5. Networking and logs

1. The App uses the Internet to call AI APIs, Drive APIs, and related services.  
2. Browser developer tools or extensions may record network traffic locally.

## 6. Attachments (images, documents)

Files you select are generally processed in-browser and may be transformed before being sent to an AI Provider. If content includes **sensitive personal data or trade secrets**, review each provider’s data-handling practices carefully.

## 7. Children

The App is not directed at children. Parental guidance is recommended. Follow local age requirements where they apply.

## 8. Security

Browser storage and network use are **not perfectly secure**. You are responsible for device security and credential management.
